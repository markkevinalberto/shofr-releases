# SHOFR — Android app

**Your phone. Your radio.** SHOFR is push-to-talk for teams: hold TALK, speak, release — everyone on the channel hears you in real time over Wi-Fi, 4G or 5G.

## Download

**[Download the latest SHOFR APK](https://github.com/markkevinalberto/shofr-releases/releases/latest/download/shofr.apk)** · or visit **https://shofr.duckdns.org/get**

No Android phone? Use the web app in Safari or Chrome: **https://shofr.duckdns.org**

## Installing

1. Open the download link on your Android phone.
2. When Android asks, allow your browser to install apps ("Install unknown apps").
3. Open SHOFR, allow the microphone, and join your team's channel with its QR code or invitation link.

The APK is signed by the SHOFR developer; later versions install over it as updates. Google Play Protect may warn about apps installed from outside the Play Store.

Voice is real time only — SHOFR does not record conversations.
